What the video shows
Beretta prices cyber insurance for Indian SMEs from graded evidence and published claims data, explains
every factor in the premium, and keeps watching the insured company's machines after the policy is bound.
The video runs the working system end to end: the underwriting platform (Part 1) and the live-monitoring
collector (Part 2), with four SIMULATED endpoints reporting through signed reports.

Chapters
1. The problem: why SME cyber insurance is priced from unverified, once-a-year answers.
2. Starting the linked demo: the platform and the collector come up together.
3. Evidence: the questionnaire and how each control is graded from DECLARED to TECHNICALLY_OBSERVED.
4. The quote: premium by peril, the effect of each control, and how much verifying a control would save.
5. Monitoring: the collector's fleet view, with every endpoint labelled SIMULATED.
6. Bind: the policy is bound and its warranties start as MET.
7. A lapse: EDR is stopped on one machine; the EDR warranty moves to IN_GRACE within seconds while the
   premium stays the same.
8. Breach and repair: the grace period is fast-forwarded (an audited demo step) to show BREACHED, then the
   machine is fixed and the warranty returns to MET. A re-price of the bound policy is refused.
9. The insured's view: a read-only page showing the premium, the evidence held and the savings per control.
10. Tests: all 224 automated tests pass across both systems.

Key point
Monitoring never re-prices a bound policy. It changes warranty states only, with a grace period first.
