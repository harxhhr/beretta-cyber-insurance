01 · The problem

Cyber insurance for firms is priced in the dark.

?

The price isn't explained

An SME gets one number. It can't see which answer raised it, or what would lower it.

Yes?

Answers are taken on trust

A "yes" on a proposal form is priced as a fact. Nobody checks it until a claim is made.

∅

Nothing is watched after bind

Policies require controls to stay in place all year. In practice nobody checks.

02 · What we built

Two linked systems, one rule between them.

PART 1 · THE PLATFORM

Underwriting you can read

Every piece of evidence is graded on a four-step ladder: declared, documented, tested, technically observed. The premium is built from published claims data, five perils at a time, and every factor is shown with its source.

PART 2 · THE MONITOR

A read-only endpoint agent

Twelve checks on Windows, Linux and macOS. Reports are signed and numbered so they can't be forged or replayed, and a collector rolls them up for the whole company.

Monitoring never re-prices a bound policy.

After bind, a failing check moves a warranty through MET → IN_GRACE → BREACHED → REMEDIATED. The premium stays exactly where it was.

04 · The demo story

EDR stops on one of four machines. Watch what moves, and what doesn't.

Premium at every step: ₹94,135

STEP 1Quoted & bound

Priced from the fleet's evidence

Four monitored machines prove six controls. The policy binds at ₹94,135.

STEP 2In grace

EDR stops on one laptop

3 of 4 pass, under the 95% bar. Within seconds the EDR warranty starts a 7-day grace period.

STEP 3Breached

Grace runs out

Still failing, the warranty is breached and its cover condition applies. The premium doesn't move.

STEP 4Met again

The fix is seen

EDR is back on. Remediated, then met, and every step sits in the audit log.

Endpoint data in the demo is SIMULATED and labelled as such in every view.
